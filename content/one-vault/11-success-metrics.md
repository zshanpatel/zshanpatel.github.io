## Overview
Success for the one-vault system is measured across **impact**, **sustainability**, and **health** dimensions. Not just growth—but meaningful, aligned growth.

**Philosophy:** What gets measured gets managed. Measure what matters.

---
## A. North Star Metric
### Primary Metric: Wealth Shares Fulfilled
**Why This Metric:**
- Measures actual utility (not just sign-ups)
- Reflects trust (people follow through)
- Captures complete cycle
- Indicates product-market fit
- Aligns with mission

```
Year 2: 1,000 fulfilled wealth shares
Year 3: 10,000 fulfilled wealth shares
Year 5: 100,000 fulfilled wealth shares
```

## J. Leading vs Lagging Indicators
### Leading Indicators (Predictive)
```
Early Warning Signs:
- Sign-up rate declining
- Engagement dropping
- Time to first share increasing
- Share creation slowing
- Support tickets rising
```
**Action:** Address immediately before affecting lagging indicators
### Lagging Indicators (Outcome)
```
Result Metrics:
- Total fulfilled shares
- Revenue
- Churn
- NPS
```
**Use:** Measure success, inform strategy

## K. Metric Pitfalls to Avoid
### Vanity Metrics
❌ **Total users** (without activity)
✅ **Active users** (actual usage)

❌ **Pageviews** (meaningless)
✅ **Wealth shares created** (value)

❌ **Social media followers** (don't use product)
✅ **Referrals leading to sign-ups** (real advocacy)
### Gaming the System
**Risk:** Optimising for metric instead of value
**Example:**
- Metric: "Wealth shares created"
- Game: Create fake shares
- Real Goal: Fulfilled shares (actual value)
**Prevention:**
- Measure complete funnels
- Track quality indicators
- User satisfaction paramount
- Mission alignment checks
