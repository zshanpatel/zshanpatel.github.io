# One-vault System
A federated, gold-pegged, peer-to-peer lending system for trusted circles.
## Overview
One-vault system restores the "one-vault economy" through digital infrastructure while maintaining full Islamic compliance. It enables interest-free, gold-backed lending within families, friends, and micro-communities.
