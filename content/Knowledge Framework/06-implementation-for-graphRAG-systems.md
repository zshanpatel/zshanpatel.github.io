### Relationship Typology
GROUNDS (Ontological Foundation)
- Higher ontological levels provide existential foundation for lower levels
- Preserves hierarchy while allowing bidirectional insight flow
- Example: Mathematics GROUNDS Physics (provides logical structure)

EMERGES_FROM (Complexity Emergence)
- Complex phenomena arising from simpler components with new properties
- Captures both reductive explanation and irreducible emergence
- Example: Consciousness EMERGES_FROM Neural_Activity

REFLECTS (Analogical Participation)
- Finite reality participating in and reflecting infinite divine attributes
- Maintains transcendence while affirming genuine similarity
- Example: Human_Justice REFLECTS Divine_Justice

TRACES_TO (Epistemological Pathway)
- Knowledge pathway connecting any concept to its ultimate divine source
- Enables systematic traversal of the hierarchy
- Example: Quantum_Mechanics TRACES_TO Al-Haqq via Mathematical_Consistency

MEDIATES (Cultural Translation)
- How universal principles become expressed in particular cultural forms
- Bridges universal and particular without reducing either
- Example: Natural_Language MEDIATES Universal_Grammar
### Advanced Query Capabilities
Ontological Depth Queries: "Trace quantum entanglement through the full hierarchy to its divine grounding"
- Returns: Physics → Mathematics → Logic → Divine Consistency (Al-Haqq)
- Includes: Philosophical implications, theological significance, practical applications

Cross-Domain Integration: "How does evolutionary biology reflect the same divine attributes as musical harmony?"
- Identifies: Common grounding in Al-Khaliq (creativity) and Al-Jameel (beauty)
- Explores: Convergent patterns, aesthetic principles, theological insights

Cultural Mediation Analysis: "Compare how different linguistic traditions express mathematical concepts"
- Examines: Universal mathematical truth through particular cultural expressions
- Reveals: Unity of truth amid diversity of expression
### Reasoning Mechanisms
Analogical Reasoning: The system can identify analogical relationships across ontological levels, recognising how the same divine attribute manifests differently at various levels of reality.

Hierarchical Inference: When information is missing at one level, the system can make principled inferences based on higher-level grounding and lower-level manifestations.

Integration Synthesis: The system can synthesise insights from multiple disciplines by tracing them to common divine sources and identifying convergent patterns.
### Theological Sophistication
Rather than treating divine attributes as mere labels, the system maintains awareness of:
- The unity and simplicity of divine being behind all attributes
- The analogical nature of all predictions about divine reality
- The cultural and linguistic mediation of all theological language
- The mystery that transcends all finite comprehension

This enables the system to engage in genuine theological reasoning while maintaining appropriate intellectual humility about the limits of finite understanding.
