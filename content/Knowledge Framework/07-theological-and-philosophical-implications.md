### Why This Framework Matters
1. Unity of Knowledge: All human knowledge forms a coherent whole rooted in divine truth
2. Sacred Science: Scientific discovery becomes a form of theological investigation
3. Intellectual Worship: Understanding creation becomes a means of knowing the Creator
4. Hierarchical Realism: Reality has genuine levels, but all are grounded in divine being
5. Linguistic Theology: Even our most abstract symbol systems point back to divine reality
### The Mystery of Language
Natural language represents the ultimate paradox: we use the most culturally-relative, historically-contingent, and abstractly-symbolic system available to us in our attempt to understand and communicate about absolute, eternal, and substantial divine truth. This creates both the possibility and the limitation of all human knowledge—we can genuinely know truth, but only through finite, mediated, symbolic forms.

This framework reveals that every word we speak, every equation we solve, every scientific discovery we make, and every philosophical insight we gain is simultaneously:
- A step further from immediate divine reality (increasing abstraction)
- A step closer to understanding divine reality (increasing comprehension)
- A reflection of divine attributes in finite form
- An invitation to recognise the divine source of all truth

In this way, all knowledge becomes a form of natural theology, and all serious intellectual work becomes a form of worship—the mind's response to the traces of divine wisdom embedded in the very structure of reality itself.
