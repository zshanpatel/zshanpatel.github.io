This framework presents a comprehensive hierarchy that traces all human knowledge back to its ultimate source: the Divine Names and Attributes of God. Like a vast tree, every piece of knowledge, from simple mathematics to complex biology has roots that extend back to divine truth.

The metaphor of a tree is particularly apt because it captures several essential features of this knowledge system:
##### 1. Unity of Source: 
Just as all branches of a tree spring from a single root system, all forms of knowledge ultimately derive from the divine source
##### 2. Hierarchical Structure: 
Knowledge becomes increasingly complex and specialized as we move from roots to branches, just as divine simplicity manifests in increasingly complex forms
##### 3. Organic Connection: 
Each level of knowledge remains connected to every other level, just as nutrients flow throughout a living tree
##### 4. Growth and Development: 
The tree of knowledge continues to grow and develop, with new discoveries extending existing branches rather than contradicting the fundamental structure
