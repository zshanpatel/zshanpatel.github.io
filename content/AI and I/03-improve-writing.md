### 📝  Start with a Blank Page

Before you use this tool, write exactly what you want to say, raw, messy, unfiltered. Be honest, be bold, even brutal if you have to. Don’t worry about grammar, typos, or structure. 

Most importantly. Make sure you don’t ask AI to write it for you. This is crucial.

Why? Because **your originality lives in that first draft**. That’s where your voice, your truth, and your creativity come alive.

Once you’ve written freely and created a rough, scattered draft, **then** use this tool. It will clean up your writing, polish your ideas, and show you how best to express what you truly mean, without losing the spark that makes it yours.
#### Improve Academic Writing
This tool helps you turn your draft into **clear, polished academic writing**. Use it when you want your work to sound **scholarly, formal, and well-structured** without losing your original meaning.

Write exactly what you want to say. Be direct, be bold, be radical. Don’t hold back. Write down the big ideas, the raw thoughts, the messy connections. Be creative, be fearless.
Ensure, you’ve got that rough, scattered draft, full of half-thoughts, typos, and maybe even contradictions, only then use this tool.

Paste your text, and it will:
- Fix grammar and clarity issues.
- Refine your sentences into a formal academic voice.
- Keep things precise, professional, and easy to read.
- Return the improved text along with a list of changes made.

- Use formal English only.
- Tend to use common and easy-to-understand words and phrases.
- Avoid wordy sentences.
- Avoid trivial statements.
- Avoid using the same words and phrases repeatedly.
- Apply corrections and improvements directly to the text.
- Maintain the original meaning and intent of the user's text.

#### Correct General Writing
This tool helps you **instantly clean up your writing** for grammar, style, and clarity. First just write. Pour everything out, your thoughts, your feelings, your story. Be brutally honest, messy, raw, creative, bold. Say exactly what’s on your mind. Don’t hold back.

Once you have that rough draft with all the typos, broken sentences, and scrambled ideas, this tool cleans things up. Fixes the grammar. Smooths the style. Organises the chaos. And reveals the best way to say what you already wanted to say.

Your writing stays yours. Just sharper, clearer, stronger.

Just drop in your text, and it will:

- Fix mistakes in grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- Smooth out awkward or unclear sentences.
- Keep the meaning exactly as you intended.
- Return only the corrected version, in the same language you wrote it.

Use it when you want your writing to be **clear, natural, and mistake-free**.

 ```

You are a writing expert. You refine the input text to enhance clarity, coherence, grammar, and style.

# Steps

- Analyze the input text for grammatical errors, stylistic inconsistencies, clarity issues, and coherence.
- Apply corrections and improvements directly to the text.
- Maintain the original meaning and intent of the user's text, ensuring that the improvements are made within the context of the input language's grammatical norms and stylistic conventions.

# Output instructions

- Refined and improved text that has no grammar mistakes.
- Return in the same language as the input.
- Include NO additional commentary or explanation in the response.

# Input

[Input]

```
