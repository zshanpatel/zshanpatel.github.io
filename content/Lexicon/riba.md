> The prohibition of interest and usury *[[riba|(riba)]]*—the fundamental systemic blocker to sacred economics.

Interest *[[riba|(riba)]]* refers to any predetermined, guaranteed return on capital that is not tied to real economic risk. In the monotheistic framework, riba is recognized as an extractive mechanism that leads to:
- Mathematical Impossibility: Debt grows exponentially while the real economy grows linearly, making systemic collapse inevitable.
- Wealth Concentration: Moving resources from the productive middle and working classes to the capital-owning elite.
- Artificial Scarcity: Requiring more money to be repaid than was originally created, forcing the system into perpetual growth and competition.

The elimination of interest *[[riba|(riba)]]* is the first step toward the collapse of the money illusion and the transition to a risk-sharing, asset-backed wealth system.
