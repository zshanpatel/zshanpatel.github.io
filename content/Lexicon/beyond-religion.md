> The recognition of Islam not as a set of private rituals, but as a comprehensive operating system for organising society, economics, and knowledge. A complete collective system.

Modernity treats "religion" as a separate silo, a private belief system that doesn't interfere with "real life" matters like politics or business. This great separation is a strategy to protect exploitative systems from moral scrutiny.

Beyond Religion reclaims the concept of a complete way of life *[[beyond-religion|(deen)]]*. [[beyond-religion|Deen]] is a total life system that integrates:
- Merged socio-spiritual development and economic justice.
- Rational inquiry and divine revelation.
- Individual agency and collective responsibility.

When we move beyond the secular reduction of "religion," we find a sophisticated framework designed to solve the grand challenges of the modern world through the integration of all dimensions of human existence.

Source: Ahmed, I. (2001). _Islamic Renaissance: The Real Task Ahead_.
