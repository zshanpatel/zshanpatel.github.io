> The principle of human stewardship *[[khalifah|(khalifah)]]*—representing the divine order on Earth through responsible action and systemic maintenance.

In the monotheistic framework, humans are not consumers or owners; we are stewards *[[khalifah|(khalifahs)]]*. This is an ontological status that defines our relationship with all of creation.

The mandate of the steward:
1. Preservation: Maintaining the natural equilibrium *[[riba|(mizan)]]* of the world.
2. Reformation: Actively dismantling extractive systems and building those that serve the public interest *[[maslahah|(maslahah)]]*.
3. Accountability: Recognizing that every action has both material consequences and spiritual weight.

When humans act as stewards *[[khalifah|(khalifahs)]]* rather than NPCs, they manifest agency and drive the transition toward universal prosperity.
