> The global community *[[ummah|(ummah)]]* and singular society characterized by collective cooperation and mutual aid across all boundaries.

The community *[[ummah|(ummah)]]* is the monotheistic vision of a "one-world" society. It is a collective based on shared values and purpose rather than race, nationality, or economic status.

Key aspects of the model:
- Global Cooperation: Transcending national and cultural borders to solve grand challenges and protect planetary stewardship.
- Mutual Aid: Built-in systems like mandatory almsgiving *[[zakat|(zakat)]]* ensure that the health of the most vulnerable is the responsibility of the most fortunate.
- Collective Consciousness: Moving from individualistic "me" thinking to collective "we" thinking, aligned with the principle of unity *[[tawhid|(tawhid)]]*.

The community *[[ummah|(ummah)]]* represents the ultimate expression of universal prosperity, where humanity acts as a single, integrated organism serving the divine order.
