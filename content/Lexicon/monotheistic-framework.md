> An epistemological architecture that organizes all knowledge, resources, and relationships around a single, unified source of truth.

The modern world is fragmented by ideological polytheism. We have separate rules for economics, separate rules for science, and separate rules for morality. This fragmentation leads to "Science without Ethics" and "Economics without Justice," resulting in systems that are internally inconsistent and socially destructive.

Unity is not a religious preference; it is a systemic necessity. A monotheistic framework (based on unity *[[tawhid|(tawhid)]]*) recognizes that reality is a coherent whole. By anchoring all systems to the divine source, we eliminate the false secular-sacred divide. This allows for a "Meta-dimensional Social Justice Movement" where economic actions are recognized as having spiritual consequences.

Implementing this framework means all decisions must pass through the universal audit: Does this action align with the divine order? This alignment creates divine blessing *[[barakah|(barakah)]]* (sustainable growth) and ensures that human agency serves as stewardship *[[khalifah|(khalifah)]]* rather than extraction.
