> One who lets the storm surround them, controls it, and embraces all its dangers and powers.

The world produces storms. Conflict, opposition, moral pressure, and the contempt of those who benefit from quiet submission. Most people are trained to avoid the storm, to seek safety in compliance, to treat disruption as failure.

The storm-bearer is the opposite. Forged through the repeated dismantling of ego priorities and the surrender of false masters, this person does not seek the storm but does not flee it either. They are recognised by some as the troublemaker, the one who will not stay quiet, who disrupts comfortable arrangements. And they are recognised by other storm-bearers as a fellow rider, someone who has also passed through the same crucible.

The transformation is not aggression. It is the natural result of ego purification and submission. When a person has been stripped of the need for approval, when lesser fears have lost their hold, the storm becomes navigable. The chaos that paralyses others becomes something the storm-bearer can move through, control, and even direct. The danger is not denied. It is embraced.
