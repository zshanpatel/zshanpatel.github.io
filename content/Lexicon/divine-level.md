> A higher category of importance, one that utility can't reach no matter how far you scale it.

Most explanations for why something matters bottom out in usefulness — it saves time, it earns money, it solves a problem. A divine-level explanation doesn't bottom out there. It asks whether the thing connects to something beyond material return at all: meaning, purpose, obligation to something outside yourself.

This is the register this essay claims AI can't currently reach — not because the question is harder, but because it isn't a utility question in the first place. It sits alongside the vault's existing framing of [[Lexicon/tawhid|unified purpose]] and [[Lexicon/khalifah|stewardship]] — both describe a level of "why" that optimisation can't produce on its own.
