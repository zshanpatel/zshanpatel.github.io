A garden is a metaphor for a lot of things: growth, persistence, and the continuation of life.

The Garden is an old metaphor associated with [[hypertext]].

Every walk through the garden creates new paths, new meanings, and when we add things to the garden we add them in a way that allows many future, unpredicted relationships.

There is no _right_ or canonical way to view it

A hard part of this is The Navigation Problem: how do we give web users just enough guidance to freely explore the web, without forcing them into pre-defined browsing experiences?
