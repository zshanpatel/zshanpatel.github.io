# 🌍 Grand Challenges (GCs)
> Large-scale, complex societal problems—such as climate change, extreme inequality, and resource overshoot—that cannot be solved through individualist or purely technical fixes.

## 🚩 The Diagnosis (The Symptom)
We view **Grand Challenges** as separate problems to be "fixed" by specialist experts. We treat malnourishment with better logistics and climate change with green energy. Yet, despite trillions in investment, these challenges continue to escalate because we are treating symptoms, not the source.

## 🔄 The Reframe (The Structural Root)
Grand Challenges are not logistics failures; they are **Moral and Epistemological Failures**. They are the inevitable result of an economic system that prioritizes extraction over circulation. You cannot solve a challenge created by infinite growth logic by using more growth-driven tech. GCs are signals from the ecosystem that the underlying "operating system" of human society is in conflict with the Divine Order.

## 💡 The Solution (The Proof)
Addressing GCs requires a shift to **Universal Prosperity**. Only a [[monotheistic-framework]] that recognizes the interdependence of all creation can provide the meta-dimensional social justice required to move from depletion to regeneration.
