# 📖 Spirituo-epistemic Source
> A foundation of knowledge that integrates divine revelation with rational inquiry to approach Truth from both material and metaphysical dimensions.

## 🚩 The Diagnosis (The Symptom)
Modernity is trapped in **Epistemological Myopia**. We believe that real knowledge only comes from what can be measured in a lab or calculated on a spreadsheet. This excludes the deepest dimensions of human meaning, purpose, and morality, leaving us with cleverness
