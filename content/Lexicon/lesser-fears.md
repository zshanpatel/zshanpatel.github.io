> The noise cleared when you submit to the only thing worth fearing.

Every person carries fears. Fear of rejection, of poverty, of failure, of being seen, of being forgotten. These fears are not random. They are the direct consequence of what a person has submitted to. What we submit to is what we fear.

The lesser fears are the noise produced by false submissions. Submit to opinion, and you fear criticism. Submit to money, and you fear loss. Submit to comfort, and you fear disruption. Each fear is a signal of the master being served, not a reflection of genuine danger.

When a person submits to God alone, the lesser fears begin to lose their charge. They do not disappear instantly, but their grip weakens. The clarity that follows is not a feeling. It is the structural result of removing false masters. Direction and purpose emerge not because a new goal was adopted, but because the noise that obscured them was silenced. Getting over your feelings, in this context, is not suppression. It is the natural consequence of submission producing bravery.
