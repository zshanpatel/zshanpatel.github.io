> The principle of public interest *[[maslahah|(maslahah)]]* and collective wellbeing as the primary driver for legal and systemic design.

Public interest *[[maslahah|(maslahah)]]* is the guiding principle for any society operating under a monotheistic framework. It recognizes that the purpose of any law, economic system, or social structure must be the preservation of:
- Faith (*din*)
- Life (*nafs*)
- Intellect (*aql*)
- Lineage (*nasl*)
- Wealth (*mal*)

When these five are protected, the community experiences universal prosperity. Maslahah is the reason we must prioritize sacred economics and community oversight *[[hisbah|(hisbah)]]*—to ensure that the common good is not sacrificed for individual or corporate gain.
