> Turning $1,000 into $100,000 — the fantasy that keeps memecoin traders in the game.

In crypto slang, "100x" means a position has multiplied in value 100 times over — a 9,900% gain. It's used two different ways: a "spot 100x" is what memecoin hunters are actually chasing, holding a token from launch until its value multiplies that far; a "100x leverage" trade is a completely different, and far more dangerous, thing — borrowed exposure 100 times your actual capital, which can be wiped out by a tiny adverse price move.

The spot version is what most of this slang evokes, and it's genuinely rare: something like one token in a thousand ever reaches 100x within two years of launch, and most never reach even a tenth of that.
