> An economic system built on divine principles of justice, circulation, and risk-sharing, rather than extraction and debt-slavery.

Modern economics is predicated on Extraction. Systems are designed to move value from the many to the few through interest *[[riba|(riba)]]* and the creation of money from nothing. This leads to systemic instability and the erosion of social trust.

**Sacred Economics** recognizes that wealth is a trust from the Divine. It operates through three core mechanisms:
1. **Risk-Sharing:** Moving from interest-based lending to partnerships where both parties share in gains and losses.
2. **Wealth Circulation:** Mandatory redistribution through almsgiving *[[zakat|(zakat)]]* to prevent hoarding and ensure the health of the entire global community *[[ummah|(ummah)]]*.
3. **Ethical Commerce:** Markets regulated by community oversight *[[hisbah|(hisbah)]]* to ensure fairness, transparency, and the prohibition of harmful products.

Economic success is measured by the elevation of the collective rather than the accumulation of the individual.
