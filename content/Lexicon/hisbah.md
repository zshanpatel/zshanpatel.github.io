> A system of community-based market oversight *[[hisbah|(hisbah)]]* and ethical regulation to ensure justice, transparency, and fair trade.

In a sacred economics framework, markets are not left to the "invisible hand" of self-interest, nor are they controlled by distant, bureaucratic regulators. Instead, they are governed by community oversight *[[hisbah|(hisbah)]]*.

Key functions of the system:
- Fair Pricing: Preventing predatory pricing, hoarding, and monopolies that exploit the vulnerable.
- Honest Trade: Ensuring the accuracy of weights and measures and the transparency of product quality (no hidden defects).
- Ethical Audit: Enforcing the prohibition of harmful products and deceptive marketing practices.
- Conflict Resolution: Providing grassroots, restorative justice for market disputes.

Market oversight *[[hisbah|(hisbah)]]* transforms the market from a site of extraction into a site of stewardship, where commerce serves the public interest *[[maslahah|(maslahah)]]* and aligns with the divine order.
