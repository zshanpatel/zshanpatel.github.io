> A systems-thinking framework that maps how change moves through different scales of reality, from individual transformation to global ecosystem shifts.

Most social movements fail because they operate only at one level. They focus on **Micro** fixes (personal habits) without changing the **Macro** system (laws/institutions), or they demand **Mundo** shifts (saving the planet) without the **Micro** spiritual grounding to sustain it. This creates fragmented, ineffective action.

Transformation is a **Multidimensional Convergence**. An MLP recognizes three inseparable levels:
1. **Micro (Individual):** Personal spiritual self-construal and *Tazkiyah*.
2. **Macro (Community/Society):** Collective consciousness and *Maslahah* (public interest).
3. **Mundo (Global/Ecosystem):** Planetary stewardship and alignment with Divine Law.
True change happens when a shift at the Micro level (Personal Awakening) aligns with a pressure at the Mundo level (Eschatological Crisis) to rupture the Macro system.

By using MLP, we stop looking for a "silver bullet" and start building **Resilient Networks**. We work on our souls while simultaneously building alternative wealth networks ([[one-vault]]) and ethical businesses ([[strive]]), knowing that these Micro and Macro efforts are part of a larger Mundo-level transition.
