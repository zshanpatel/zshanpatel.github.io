> A person whose actions, thoughts, and emotions are automated by external systems, cultural programming, and unexamined impulses.

An NPC is a metaphorical concept for an individual who has surrendered their agency to the surrounding environment. Their decisions are not deliberate; they are reactions.

Key signs of NPC behavior:
- Automated Reaction: Responding to marketing, social pressure, and tribal cues without logic-auditing the intent.
- Flawed Idea of Self: Believing they are the "main character" while being a puppet to systemic conditioning.
- Mental Prisons: Operating from inherited thought patterns (ideologies) that they did not choose and cannot see.

To break the NPC state, one must engage in purification *[[tazkiyah|(tazkiyah)]]* and reclaim their agency through the ontological reframe—moving from "doing" to "being" and finally to acting as a steward *[[khalifah|(khalifah)]]* of reality.
