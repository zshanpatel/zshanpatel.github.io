> A foundational spirituo-epistemic source of knowledge that integrates divine revelation with rational inquiry to approach truth from both material and metaphysical dimensions.

Modernity is trapped in epistemological myopia. We believe that real knowledge only comes from what can be measured in a lab or calculated on a spreadsheet. This excludes the deepest dimensions of human meaning, purpose, and morality, leaving us with cleverness without wisdom.

Intrinsic epistemology recognizes that truth is not just discovered through external observation, but also perceived through the heart *[[tazkiyah|(qalb)]]* and guided by revelation. This source of knowledge provides the absolute anchor necessary to navigate the complexity of the modern world without falling into compound ignorance.
