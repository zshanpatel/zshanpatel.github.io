## The Hermeneutical Circle
The deepest insight of this framework is its circular nature:
1. We begin with God (pure truth, absolute reality)
2. We descend through increasing layers of abstraction and cultural mediation
3. We arrive at natural language (maximum abstraction: pure symbol and convention)
4. Yet we use this most abstract tool to try to understand and return to God

This creates what theologians call the hermeneutical circle: we must use finite, culturally-bound language to apprehend infinite, transcendent truth.

Rather than being a philosophical problem, this circularity reveals the profound mystery of human knowledge: we are finite beings capable of genuine insight into infinite reality. The circle is not vicious but revelatory. It shows how divine reality communicates itself through the very structure of creation and consciousness.
