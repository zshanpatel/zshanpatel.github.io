# One-Vault System
**Open source, self-hostable, federated wealth sharing for trusted circles.**
## Overview
One-vault system restores the "one-vault economy" through digital infrastructure while maintaining full Islamic compliance. It enables interest-free, gold-backed lending within families, friends, and micro-communities.

**Key Attributes:**
- ✅ **Open Source** (Apache 2.0 license)
- ✅ **Self-hostable** (run on your own server)
- ✅ **Cloud Option** (we also offer managed hosting)
- ✅ **Islamic Compliant** (*qard al-hassan*, no interest)
- ✅ **Gold-Pegged** (inflation-proof)
- ✅ **Federated** (independent circles)
## Two Ways to Use

### 1. Self-Host (Free Forever)
Download the code, run on your own server. Unlimited users, unlimited circles, all features. Perfect for tech-savvy users or organisations with IT teams.
### 2. Cloud Hosted (Freemium)
We manage everything for you. Free tier available, optional paid upgrades for advanced features. Perfect for individuals and families.
