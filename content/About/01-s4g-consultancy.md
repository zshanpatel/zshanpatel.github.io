### S4G Consultancy — Brand Solutions Director
**Mar 2022 – Present | London / Remote**

S4G's (Strive for Growth) work sits at the intersection of strategy consulting, [[Systems and AI/index|AI systems design]], and [[Lexicon/theory-u|organisational transformation]].

What this role actually involves:

- **[[02 Documents/S4G Knowledge Base/s4g-tech-spec|Context architecture]]**: Designing the specification layer between organisational intent and AI-executable action. In practice: mapping what an organisation knows, identifying where that knowledge is trapped in documents or silos, and building systems that let it move.
- **Strategic landscape analysis**: Using [[Systems and AI/index|mapping and systems thinking]] to help clients see where their organisation sits, where the industry is heading, and what moves are available to them that competitors cannot yet see.
- **[[Systems and AI/index|AI implementation strategy]]**: Working with organisations on how to deploy AI systems responsibly, specifying what persistent [[06 Metadata/agents|agents]] do autonomously, what they flag for human review, and what they never touch.
- **Research-to-strategy translation**: Converting academic research, qualitative insight, and market intelligence into [[06 Metadata/root-index|decision frameworks]] that executives can actually act from.
- **Thought leadership and positioning**: Developing the intellectual infrastructure ([[Lexicon/index|frameworks]], [[Essays/index|essays]], case studies) that establishes credibility in niche strategy and AI positioning.

Clients span C-suite advisory, early-stage venture strategy, and public sector transformation briefs.
