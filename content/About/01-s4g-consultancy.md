### Brand Solutions Director at S4G Consultancy
**Mar 2022 – Present | London / Remote**

S4G's (Strive for Growth) work sits at the intersection of strategy consulting, [[Systems and AI/index|AI systems design]], and [[civilisational-transformation|organisational transformation]].

What this role actually involves:

- **Context architecture**: Designing the specification layer between organisational intent and AI-executable action. In practice: mapping what an organisation knows, identifying where that knowledge is trapped in documents or silos, and building systems that let it move.
- **Strategic landscape analysis**: Using [[Systems and AI/index|mapping and systems thinking]] to help clients see where their organisation sits, where the industry is heading, and what moves are available to them that competitors cannot yet see.
- **[[Systems and AI/index|AI implementation strategy]]**: Working with organisations on how to deploy AI systems responsibly, specifying what persistent agents do autonomously, what they flag for human review, and what they never touch.
- **Research-to-strategy translation**: Converting academic research, qualitative insight, and market intelligence into decision frameworks that executives can actually act from.
- **Thought leadership and positioning**: Developing the intellectual infrastructure ([[Lexicon/index|frameworks]], [[Essays/index|essays]], case studies) that establishes credibility in niche strategy and AI positioning.

Clients span C-suite advisory, early-stage venture strategy, and public sector transformation briefs, including a [[03-hackney|digital transformation engagement for the London Borough of Hackney]], delivered in association with UCL's [[04-ucl|Institute for Global Prosperity]].
