The banks [[Lexicon/illusion-money|create wealth from nothing]]. Most money doesn't actually exist. Understanding this single fact could free you from [[Lexicon/economic-slavery|financial slavery]].

Take out your wallet. Look at your money. What you're holding isn't wealth, it's an [[Lexicon/iou|IOU]] (I owe you). A promise. A piece of paper (or digital entry) that has value only because everyone agrees to pretend it has value.

And most money isn't even printed. It's created by typing numbers into a computer. When you take out a loan, banks don't lend you money that someone else deposited. They create new money out of nothing, loan it to you, and then [[Lexicon/usury|charge you interest]] on money that didn't exist until the moment they approved your loan.

You work real hours to pay interest on imaginary money.

John Perkins, in "Confessions of an [[Lexicon/economic-hitman|Economic Hitman]]," revealed how this system works on a global scale. But the same principles apply to you:

Step 1: Create Money from Nothing. Banks literally type numbers into computers. No gold backing. No vault full of cash. Just digits on a screen.
Step 2: Lend It at Interest. They charge real interest on this imaginary money. The interest is always real, even when the principal is fictional.
Step 3: Demand Real [[Lexicon/collateral|Collateral]]. When you can't pay back their fake money with interest, they make you pay old loans with new loans and impose liberal values. 
Step 4: Repeat Globally. This system has been scaled up to [[Lexicon/structural-adjustment|enslave entire countries through un-payable debt]].

It's the most elegant con in human history.

This is how modern debt-money system works:

Traditional Economics Fairy Tale: Banks take deposits from savers and lend them to borrowers, earning profit on the interest spread.
Actual Reality: Banks create money when they make loans, destroy money when loans are repaid, and create [[Lexicon/artificial-scarcity|artificial scarcity]] to maintain control.

When you borrow $100,000 for a house:
- The bank types "$100,000" into your account (money created from nothing)
- You owe $100,000 plus interest (let's say $150,000 total over 30 years)
- But the extra $50,000 for interest was never created, it doesn't exist in the money supply
- This creates a mathematical impossibility: there's never enough [[Lexicon/money-supply|money in circulation]] for everyone to pay back their loans with interest

The system requires most people to be in debt forever.
## The Plutocratic Control System (Governed by the Wealthy) 
Ann Pettifor's research reveals how this creates what she calls "[[Lexicon/plutocratic-finance|plutocratic finance]]": rule by the wealthy through control of money creation.

Here's the [[Lexicon/power|power structure]]:
1. [[Lexicon/central-bank|Central Banks]] create base money and lend it to major financial institutions
2. Major Banks create most money through lending and control who gets [[Lexicon/access-to-credit|access to credit]]
3. Everyone Else competes for access to money that others control

This is the free market [[Lexicon/financial-feudalism|financial feudalism]].

[[Lexicon/usury|Usury]] (charging of interest) creates systemic problems :

Exponential Growth in Finite Systems. Interest compounds exponentially, but real wealth (goods and services) grows much more slowly. This mathematical mismatch creates inevitable [[Lexicon/boom-and-bust-cycles|boom and bust cycles]].

[[Lexicon/wealth-concentration|Wealth Concentration]]. Interest payments flow from borrowers to lenders, systematically transferring wealth from the working class to the capital class.

Artificial Scarcity. Because banks create the principal but not the interest, there's never enough money in circulation for everyone to pay back their debts.

Economic Slavery. Individuals, businesses, and entire countries become trapped in cycles of debt that are mathematically impossible to escape.
