Guys like me bro, we are at the bottom of the [[wealth-concentration|wealth concentration]] bro.
Apply for jobs on a million [[job-portals|portals]].
We can get the best [[education|education]].
Run our [[entrepreneurship|enterprises]].
[[meritocracy|Rank]] as hard as we can.
Pitch to a hundred places.
Do the work that three people in the room couldn't.

Muslim. Brown. Men?
Forget about it. 

You will only always reach n-1 of the n we work for. Every time.
This is the hard truth.
Every time, you hold a position. You were [[overqualification|overqualified]] for.
Every job, you are [[overwork|overworked]] for.

What even is [[social-entrepreneurship|social entrepreneurship]]? What? Sell sugar to children and call it solving hunger? What? 

Who decided what [[social-cause|social cause]] was worth it?
Who allowed you to charge for a social cause?
You say [[grand-challenges|climate change]], I see [[carbon-credits|carbon credits]].
[[rebranding|Rebrand]] and [[extraction|extract]].

Made to forget. To be afraid to even hint [[divine-moral-supremacy|divine moral supremacy]]. The highest system. 
The truth is that, the [[social-balance|social balance]] is not in our favour.
And I am not victimising myself here. Quite the opposite. 

Activate the superior system we have, in play.
We had [[just-systems|just systems]] in place.
In the [[islamic-golden-age|golden age]], Muslim brown men ruled this earth. 
But the corruption rotted our hearts.
It still infects our soul.
The systems established. Planning, demanding our surrender, our [[obedience|obedience]] to this deceptive mediocre [[technocracy|technocracy]]. 
Where men like us are but [[npc|cogs]] in their grand machine.

This the [[law-of-the-jungle|jungle]] and we have been brought to our knees. 

But the [[law-of-the-jungle|law of the jungle]] dictates: the lion eats the grazing sheep.
And it can only be [[caged-lions|caged]] for so long. 

Basic [[civilisational-transformation|civilisation cycle]] 101. 
You get rich, you get fat, you get lazy, 
The desperate get hungry, then they hunt.
The hungry always eat the comfortable. Repeat. The cycle resets.
History rhymes, every time.

The jungle doesn't negotiate.
