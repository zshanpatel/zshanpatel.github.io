## Understanding God's System
Everything we think we know about "religion" is keeping from discovering the most sophisticated [[Lexicon/beyond-religion|life-system]] ever developed.

By secular design. When most people hear "God," they immediately think "religion" and their brains switch off. 

The word "religion" comes from Latin "religio," meaning "to bind" or "constrain." It suggests something separate from "real life": a private belief system that shouldn't interfere with practical matters like economics, politics, or social organisation. This separation is one of the greatest con job in intellectual history.
