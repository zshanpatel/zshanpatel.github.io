Ancient traditions had sophisticated financial alternatives to an [[Lexicon/riba|interest-based]] system.

[[Lexicon/risk-sharing|Risk-sharing]] preferred over of interest-based debts. Instead of guaranteed returns for lenders, finance was based in [[Lexicon/mudarabah|profit-and-loss sharing arrangements]] where both parties share risks and rewards.

[[Lexicon/asset-backed-money|Asset-backed]]. Money was backed by real assets (gold, silver) or represented claims on real goods, preventing the creation of [[Lexicon/illusion-money|money from nothing]].

Excessive uncertainty *([[Lexicon/gharar|Gharar]])* was prohibited, preventing the kind of financial gambling that creates economic instability.

[[Lexicon/waqf|Community-based banking]]. Financial institutions served community needs rather than maximising profits for distant shareholders.

The present [[Lexicon/halal-washing|ethical banks]] have been corrupted by the same system they were meant to replace. They've created complex legal workarounds that technically avoid interest while replicating all the same effects.

A true finance system would fundamentally restructure the entire monetary system. What we have instead are conventional banks with ethical branding and religious marketing.

So why do we accept this system? Because we've been [[Lexicon/npc|psychologically conditioned]] to believe several myths:

Myth: Interest reflects [[Lexicon/time-value-of-money|time value]]
Reality: Interest is a wealth transfer mechanism disguised as compensation for time.

Myth: Banking is a service
Reality: Modern banking is wealth extraction disguised as service.

Myth: This System is Natural
Reality: Debt-money systems are recent historical inventions. For most of human history, money was asset-based.

The financial system we've been trying so hard to succeed in is fundamentally rigged. The game is designed to keep you running on a treadmill, generating real value to pay interest on imaginary money.
