This is the most hyped AI product: automating processes using AI. A lot of AI agencies are selling these apart from [[Lexicon/chatbot|chatbots]]: personal assistants, [[Lexicon/lead-generation|lead generation]], email management, calendar scheduling.
I set up a few automations for myself but I'm yet to close in on what I actually need agents for. And it seems like every day there are new [[Lexicon/ai-agent|AI agents]] with more advanced functionalities. Very interesting and entertaining space. 

I would compare this to graphic design tools in the 90s or social media management in the 2000s. While these tools were all the rage, the real value remained at the [[Lexicon/macro-strategy|macro-strategy]] level.

Anyway, I bet most [[Lexicon/influencer|influencers]], are making more from their [Skool](https://www.skool.com/) communities than actually selling these agents. But there's still scope for specialisation.
### What Sells
The intelligent chatbot is actually the easiest and most sold product. It takes some back and forth with the client to understand what the customer is looking for, but programming a simple meeting scheduler or AI assistant is not the hard part. The challenge is building the appropriate [[Lexicon/knowledge-base|knowledge base]] and [[Lexicon/ux|user experience]]. If someone wants to buy for their business, it can be built.

What we need to do first is create '[[Lexicon/ecosystems|ecosystems]]'. That is an offering.
#### Strategic Thinking
Create business outcomes. The cut-paste solutions of just automation crafts are just tools. Showing a demo of an ecosystem, now that's a working [[Lexicon/use-case|use case]].

Example of an automation: a social media agent that emails you content it's going to post for you. You reply "approved" and it's done, on all your platforms. Or reply with "make these changes" and it sends back revised content.

Example of an ecosystem: builds a business solution that uses multiple digital avenues to transform outputs for business. Here the focus is on [[Lexicon/wardley-mapping|evolving technology]]. [[Lexicon/build-vs-buy|Build versus buy]]. Evolving tech like real-time data use. 
Basically, for businesses, [[Lexicon/roi|return on investment]], efficiency and ease of use and [[Lexicon/ui|user interface]] that will matter eventually.
### Where We Are Now
Creating AI Agents or "digital workers." These are software applications designed to do tasks and share the workload. For example, that social media agent I mentioned. Once set up, all you need to do is approve the content. Like an [[Lexicon/agency-model|agency model]], understanding the basics of how you can use AI and keep selling it. The next step would be to create a [[Lexicon/saas|SaaS]].
