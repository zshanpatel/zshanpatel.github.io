# Prompt Library

This page indexes all available prompts in the Company Brain kit. Each prompt is designed to help you handle a specific task, from organising files and documenting workflows to drafting and refining content. To use a prompt, open its file, copy the prompt block, and paste it into your AI assistant.

## Getting Started

These prompts help you set up your operational foundation.

| Prompt | Description |
|---|---|
| [Organise Your Knowledge](10-organise-knowledge.md) | Turn scattered notes, docs, and files into a clean, searchable system. |
| [Document a Process](11-document-a-process.md) | Turn a messy "how we do X" into a clear, step-by-step standard operating procedure. |
| [Brief Your AI Assistant](12-brief-your-ai.md) | Write a clear context brief so your AI assistant understands your work, your preferences, and your constraints. |
