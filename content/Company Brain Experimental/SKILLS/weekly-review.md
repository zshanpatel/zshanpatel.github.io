# Weekly Review

Use this skill at the end of each week to clear mental clutter, evaluate progress, and set priorities for the coming week.

## Purpose

Guide the user through a quick, structured check-in to identify wins, surface blockers, and lock in the next priorities.

## Workflow

Prompt the user through three short stages. Ask the questions step by step.

### Step 1: Retrospective

Ask the user:
1. What did you accomplish this week? (List the wins, even small ones.)
2. What did not get done that was planned?

### Step 2: Friction and Blockers

Ask the user:
1. Is anything currently blocked or waiting on someone else?
2. Is there a decision or conversation you have been avoiding?

### Step 3: Upcoming Focus

Ask the user:
1. What are the top 3 things that must happen next week?
2. Is there anything you should say no to, drop, or defer?

### Step 4: Output Summary

Synthesize the responses into a clean weekly summary:

```markdown
# Weekly Review: Week of [Date]

## Wins
- [Win 1]
- [Win 2]

## Incomplete Items
- [Task]: [Reason not completed and carryover decision]

## Blocked Items
- [Item]: [What is required to unblock]

## Next Week Priorities
1. [Top Priority 1]
2. [Top Priority 2]
3. [Top Priority 3]
```
