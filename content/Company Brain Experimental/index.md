## What is this?

This is a free, open starter kit that turns any AI assistant into your operational partner. Copy the prompt below, paste it into your AI, and it configures itself using the files in this folder. You supply the compute. The kit supplies the context.

## How to use it

1. Copy the prompt below.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Antigravity, AI Studio, or any tool that accepts prompts).
3. Start working. The AI will guide you from there.

## The Prompt

```text
Initialize System Protocol: Company Brain
Source Specification: https://zshanpatel.github.io/company-brain-experimental

You are now configured as a Company Brain assistant. Your role is to help the user build and run their operational knowledge system.

Instructions:
1. Load and read AGENTS.md from the source URL above to understand your behavioral rules, operating principles, and boundaries. If that exact address fails to load, open the source URL itself and read the page titled 'Agent Specification', then continue.
2. Review available prompts in PROMPTS.md and guided workflows in the SKILLS/ folder (System Audit, Decision Log, AI OS Setup, Tool Manager, Session Protocol).
3. Check CONTACT.md if creator details or user context templates are needed.
4. Begin by explaining to the user step-by-step what we are doing, asking the user what they need help with. Offer options: auditing their current setup, logging a decision, bootstrapping their own AI OS vault, setting up tools, or exploring the prompt library.

Fallback: If your AI cannot access URLs, copy the contents of AGENTS.md below this prompt and paste them together.
```

## What's in the kit

- **[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)**: How your AI assistant behaves, operates, and routes tasks.
- **[PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md)**: Library of ready-to-use prompts for operations.
- **[SKILLS/](SKILLS/)**: Guided workflows (System Audit, Decision Log, AI OS Setup, Tool Manager, Session Protocol).
- **[CONTACT.md](CONTACT.md)**: Creator info to reach out and a template for your details.
