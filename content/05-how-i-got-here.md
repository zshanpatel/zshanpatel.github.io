Personal development is finishing you.

I think personal development is a lie. Maybe personal redevelopment.

Hear me out. In no way am I saying that you should not develop yourself. In fact I have spent a majority of my life trying to one-up myself. Trying to build myself, physically, mentally (the memory building exercises or reading the secret or just the subtle art of not giving a fuck) or emotionally. 

I am saying that, the area of focus of the 'self' itself goes against the natural progression to the divine end. I am a believer. I believe life has a beginning and an end. We come from God, and we will return to him. This is not sad or depressing, this is just facts. And we must look at life as temporary. And long temporary period that begs purpose.

So why is personal development a lie? Because the unsaid rule of personal development is the focus on the ego. I must get rich. I must have 20 bodies. Or I must live an Instagram worthy lifestyle. The focus on the self, cuts us off of the divine end. The moment you accept the phenomena of you versus the world, you are essentially cutting off God from the equation and are going down the rabbit hole aligning yourself to life of focus on what serves your ego, of material goals as opposed to life of purpose that serves the afterlife. And this, this zone of thought is where lay await the sharks of capital. 

Remember, they want your money. They cannot stop now, they are invested. Money is the name of the game. And the truth of the society we live in, is that the flow if money if only one way. Up. No longer do concepts of re-distribution of wealth have any meaning left. I almost always cringe on the thought of words like sustainability, or ethics or morality. Every time I give some input to ChatGPT or Claude or any of the LLMs (Large Language Models), they throw these social concepts in a box so casually and give back these unsatisfactory half-lies that are so well placed for this secular circle of hyperconsumption. You try building one of these concepts in the image of pre-industrial revolution and come back and tell me what your experience was like. I will tell you now. You will come back defeated and doubting yourself if ever this really made sense. Again. I am not against the use of AI (Artificial Intelligence) or anything. I am just trying to paint a picture of how we got here.
