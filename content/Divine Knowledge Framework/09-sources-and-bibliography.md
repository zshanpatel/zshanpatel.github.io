## Primary Theological Sources
### Islamic Sources:
- Al-Ghazali, Abu Hamid. The Revival of the Religious Sciences (Ihya Ulum al-Din). Translated by T.J. Winter. Cambridge: Islamic Texts Society, 1995.
- Ibn Sina (Avicenna). The Metaphysics of The Healing (Al-Shifa). Translated by Michael Marmura. Provo: Brigham Young University Press, 2005.
- Ibn Rushd (Averroes). The Decisive Treatise (Fasl al-Maqal). Translated by Charles Butterworth. Provo: Brigham Young University Press, 2001.
- Al-Farabi. Perfect State (Al-Madina al-Fadila). Translated by Richard Walzer. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1985.
- Ibn Arabi. The Meccan Revelations (Al-Futuhat al-Makkiyya). Translated by William Chittick. New York: Pir Press, 2002.
- Al-Ash'ari, Abu al-Hasan. The Theology of Al-Ash'ari. Translated by Richard McCarthy. Beirut: Imprimerie Catholique, 1953.
### Christian Sources:
- Aquinas, Thomas. Summa Theologica. Translated by the Fathers of the English Dominican Province. New York: Benziger Brothers, 1947. Available online at newadvent.org. 
- Aquinas, Thomas. Summa Contra Gentiles. Translated by Anton C. Pegis. Notre Dame: University of Notre Dame Press, 1975.
- Augustine. Confessions. Translated by R.S. Pine-Coffin. London: Penguin Classics, 1961.
- Augustine. The City of God. Translated by Henry Bettenson. London: Penguin Classics, 1972.
- Pseudo-Dionysius. The Complete Works. Translated by Colm Luibheid. New York: Paulist Press, 1987.
- Eckhart, Meister. The Complete Works. Translated by Edmund Colledge and Bernard McGinn. New York: Paulist Press, 1981.
### Jewish Sources:
- Maimonides, Moses. Guide for the Perplexed. Translated by M. Friedländer. London: George Routledge & Sons, 1904.
- Saadia Gaon. The Book of Beliefs and Opinions. Translated by Samuel Rosenblatt. New Haven: Yale University Press, 1948.
- Philo of Alexandria. The Works of Philo. Translated by C.D. Yonge. Peabody: Hendrickson Publishers, 1993.
