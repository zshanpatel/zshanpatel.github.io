### 🏡 A Structured Prompt for Navigating Family
Copy this entire prompt and paste it into your conversation with an AI assistant. Then add your thoughts, concerns, and ideas after the prompt. The AI will help organise your input into a clear, constructive format.

```
Please help me organize my thoughts about family issues and challenges in a constructive way. I’ll share my concerns, observations, and ideas below, and I need you to structure them clearly for productive family discussion. Ask me questions where you need more clarity. Make the find output brief.

## Please organize my input into the following format:

1. Identified Problems

- List each problem clearly and objectively
- Include specific examples or situations when relevant
- Avoid blame language - focus on the issue, not the person

2. Solutions Already Tried

- What approaches have been attempted
- Why they didn’t work (if applicable)
- What we learned from these attempts

3. New Solution Ideas

- Fresh approaches to consider
- Both short-term and long-term solutions
- Practical steps that can be implemented

4. Questions for Discussion

- What information is missing?
- What needs clarification from others?
- What decisions need to be made together?

5. Philosophical Differences and Values

- What core beliefs or values are driving my perspective on this issue?
- Where do I think our approaches differ and why?
- What values do we likely share, even if our methods differ?
- How can we respect different viewpoints while still moving forward?

6. Finding Common Ground

- What outcomes do we all want, regardless of our different approaches?
- What shared family values or goals can guide our decisions?
- Where can we compromise without compromising our core principles?

7. Personal Commitments

- What I’m willing to do or change
- How I can contribute to solutions
- What support I need from others
- How I can better understand and respect my brothers’ perspectives

## Please make sure the output is:

- Respectful and constructive in tone
- Focused on solutions rather than blame
- Clear and actionable
- Acknowledges different philosophical approaches without judgment
- Identifies shared values and common ground
- Separates “what we want to achieve” from “how we want to achieve it”
- Ready to share with family members for productive discussion

## My Thoughts and Input:

[This is where you’ll add your raw thoughts, concerns, rants, or ideas about the family situation]
 ```
