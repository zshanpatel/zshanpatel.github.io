Here is the story of a boy who grew up without feels. His father believed, he was destined to be great, so the father always gave his son the best he could manage and raised him to pursue nothing less. His mother always wanted to be sure nothing ever hurt her son on his endless ambitious pursuit, so she taught him to be cautious. Ambition was everything to him and emotions were a crutch for the weak. So, he never bothered.

He was mostly by himself and all he cared about was his family and few friends he called family. He had developed ideals that very few could match so he kept them close when he found them and couldn’t care less about the rest. 

Years passed, he had watched people come and go, but he was too sure about people he cared about, that they would always be by his side like he had decided to be by theirs. He had figured out how to pick and choose and look past their flaws. The boy thought he had figured it all out.

One day, a girl told him he was hers and he believed her because he wanted to. He had been in love with her from the moment he met her. He tried to hide it but he never could. She was everything he had imagined. Someone who brightened his gloomy world. Someone he thought he didn’t deserve. The girl walked through parts of his core he didn’t know existed. He let her dissect his heart just to know how it felt to feel. He prayed that she never found the emotionless peasant that hid inside him. But she did.

She walked out, leaving him with an open chest and a hole that he could never fill. The boy tried. He blamed his innocence for his shortfall. He thought if only he could forger, maybe he could regain the happiness he had found and now lost. Nothing was ever enough to fill the void that she had left him with. He only found unending emptiness at the end of every line and silent giggles at the bottom of all the empty bottles.

Over the years, the pain kept pegging him like a pebble in the shoe. All he wanted to do was forget and all she wanted was to make sure he remembered her in good faith. Every time he thought he had built a wall around his feeble heart, she would smile at him and the wall would fall to pieces. She never realized what she was doing to him and he was too proud to tell her she was hurting him. So, he pretended to go on about life. He would build his wall and she would shatter it.

Now a man, he acts humble, strong and hard. His eyes tell a different story.
