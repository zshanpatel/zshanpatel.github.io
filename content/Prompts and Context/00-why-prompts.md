## 🪴 Getting Started

**Context** gives AI the background it needs to understand your situation. Without it, responses are generic. With it, they're tailored to your specific needs, constraints, and goals.

**Prompts** are your steering wheel. A vague prompt gets vague results. A clear, specific prompt—with details about format, tone, audience, and purpose gets exactly what you need.

Together, they transform AI from a basic question-answering tool into a precision instrument that delivers relevant, actionable results on the first try.

Think of it this way: context is _who you are and what you're doing_, prompts are _what you want and how you want it_. Both are essential for quality output.

Fine-tuned prompts allow snipping the perfect user message each time.

I have collected a set of prompts that get you off the ground fast and soaring to the first floor.
