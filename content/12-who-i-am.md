When I was in 12th grade I used to say Saudi sucks dude, and I went to India, then after living there for 4 years, I was like India sucks too, then I moved to Saudi, then I realised location doesn’t matter, it is me and my escapist mindset by which I am limiting myself. It took me 3 location changes to realise it, Shan just doesn’t want to realise that and is still in that mindset, if only this happens then that will happen.

 I am increasingly becoming apprehensive of these “lines drawn by humans” called borders and their need for jumping hoops to get a fancy piece of paper that define which lines you can call your “own”- as a futile concept that should not apply to us.
We can do anything with a laptop and wifi. Anything. But we want to sit and talk about things which are irrelevant

From what I have seen and the kind of degenerates I have seen. These kind of motivations give me chills now. Old men, eating shit food, watching TV, alone, smoking cigarettes, talking big, shit slave lives, scrambling for cheap money. Delusion bro

Can’t do anything because I want to go to this location. Can’t go to that location because I don’t have capital. System shut down.

We are in this LKG level discussion for ages now. We are not even graduating from this pointless round-and-round. It doesn’t matter what location you are in. YOu have to make the best of what is now and in the present and stop this ‘if only I was in a different location’ nonsense. You are just blocking your own self.

I don’t want to play this comparison nonsense because it doesn’t go anywhere. 

Because it is not useful. Every time you want to do something. Oh it will cost this. Let me not do it. The end. Our attention is precious. Every view and moment has to be well spent. Not on some rubbish telling you ohhh you need this much money and that much money or you can’t do something.

I’ve been living this one room, shared, greedy landlord, noisy, shit life with shit food expecting something good to happen to make all this worth it. I think I need to recalibrate. I’ve burned myself out. I want to stop expecting something to happen and live in the present with what I have. Dignified and comfortable. 
Aagey, Inshallah once I calm my head, I’ll figure what I can do. If I come back, I’m not coming back for some undignified odd job. I have to figure out how I can bring income without putting myself through these situations now. I am not alone. There is a factory of sad old men here with these motivations. Just watching TV and counting days
