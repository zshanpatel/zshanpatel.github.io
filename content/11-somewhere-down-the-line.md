Somewhere down the line, I misunderstood the purpose of life. 

The idea of people is weird. Back in Saudi, everything felt so peaceful like there was no noise on the streets, no honking, nobody yelling at each other unless provoked, nobody provoking each other, no one feeling the need to provoke each other, everything seemed so calm, clean and organized, just perfect. It’s like these two drastically polar worlds; Saudi and India. Both people from these worlds would never get along. And me stuck in limbo, couldn’t confine to one box or the other so I made my own little world. Never belonged, always curious and forever seeking something.

I was happier then. I wasn’t any less miserable but happy people made it feel better. The abundance of everything that I took for granted, till those people faded away in memory one after the other. The world I want to go back to doesn’t exist anymore, maybe it never did, and maybe it was just a figment of my imagination. Now over the years all this dread has slowly become a part of me. Now I find myself maneuvering through this chaos. I always end up day dreaming about getting away from it all, sometimes not sure if it is about this place, this country or life. 

I find myself at peace and motivated only in high stakes situation. The mundane drives me crazy. Like I have no control when I am stable. Forever lost in this maze of productivity and ambition, I feel like I have lost sight of the bigger picture; to be human and emote. I let dreams of power overshadow everything else. It is the normal that drives me mad. I find myself craving something new, the next, an adventure, some adrenaline, a connection, just some time outside my head, a few laughs. We used to laugh so much but now it seems so pointless. 

Holding small talk conversations make me feel like running for the hills. I would love to get into deep talks, but now I think about how much I drain myself to open up like that to every person, with whom I try to desperately build something and fail like it was just not meant to be. I know I should stop calculating everything and just walk in the metaphorical garden, my feet touching the wet green grass, where there are no fences and thoughts can roam free without the need to be controlled.

Maybe if I didn’t like being alone, I would actually act and or react in a way that would work in my favor. But again and again, I find myself chasing the impossible. Self-destructing at the first chance I get. Happiness is so hard to handle, the only way I can have any control over it is to destroy it and finally feel at peace, alone by myself. Actually feeling a sense of relief in pushing everything and everyone away so I can finally feel at peace in my own mind. I often find myself wondering if this is fair. But life isn’t fair.
